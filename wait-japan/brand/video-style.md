# WAIT, JAPAN? — Video Design System v1

## Brand idea
A modern illustrated documentary that feels like a Japanese editorial print brought to life — not a generic AI slideshow.

## Palette
- Deep navy: #082D43
- Warm paper: #F8F1E6
- Vermilion: #E63946
- Muted blue-gray: #6C8AA3
- Charcoal ink: #20272B

## Visual language
- Warm off-white paper texture
- Strong navy ink shapes and line work
- Vermilion used only for questions, dates, arrows and reveals
- Ukiyo-e / Showa editorial influence without copying existing artworks
- Flat layered depth, subtle parallax, paper-cut transitions
- One visual metaphor per scene
- Question mark is the recurring brand symbol

## Motion rules
- Prefer 2–5 second visual beats
- Use pans, reveals, cut-paper wipes, map/timeline movement and object animation
- Avoid endless Ken Burns zooms on static AI images
- Avoid repeating identical camera moves scene after scene
- Historical scenes should communicate evidence/timeline, not fake photorealistic footage

## Typography
Large English headline, maximum 3–7 words on screen. Captions should be readable on mobile and should not duplicate every spoken word when a shorter visual label communicates the point.

## Long-form thumbnail v1
Image: clean Tokyo street + visitor holding empty cup + conspicuously empty space where a bin should be.
Text: **NO TRASH CANS?**
Secondary visual: one large vermilion question mark.
Do not place the full video title on the thumbnail.

## Originality guardrail
Branding may repeat. Substance may not. Every episode must introduce a new researched question, distinct story arc, scene-specific visuals, and its own evidence map.
