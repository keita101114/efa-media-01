What this tool does

WAIT JAPAN Content Automation is a private, internal command-line tool used by its owner to manage video publishing workflows for channels that the owner controls.

The tool uses the YouTube Data API Services to perform authorized tasks such as:

  • uploading creator-owned video files;
  • setting and updating titles, descriptions, tags, and other metadata;
  • uploading thumbnails;
  • checking video upload and processing status;
  • verifying the target channel before publishing actions; and
  • setting publication visibility and scheduling when explicitly approved by the owner.

Access and authorization

This tool is not offered to the general public. It has no public login or demo account. Access to YouTube data is authorized through Google OAuth 2.0 by the owner of the relevant channel.

The tool is designed so that publishing actions remain under the channel owner's control. It verifies the intended channel before performing upload or update operations, and public release decisions require explicit owner approval.

Purpose

The purpose of the tool is to reduce repetitive manual work in a creator's content-production workflow while preserving creator control over channel selection, video visibility, metadata, and publication decisions.
